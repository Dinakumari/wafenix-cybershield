Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry or booking request, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, company, phone number, preferred meeting date and time, and message.

How we use it

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, review service requirements, communicate about booking requests, and provide requested services.

Storage

Enquiries and booking requests are stored in the website's SQLite database so authorised administrators can manage them. Keep your hosting environment and administrator credentials secure.

Sharing

We do not sell submitted contact information. Information may be shared with service providers only when needed to operate the website or fulfil a requested service.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@wafenixcybershield.com.
